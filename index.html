      From your first offer to your own domain

      How direct purchases work

      You agree the purchase directly with the owner. Payment and completion are handled through an independent escrow service.

      01

      Agree the details

      Send your offer. We confirm availability and agree the price, any applicable tax, fees and transfer terms in writing.

      02

      Fund the escrow

      Review the agreed transaction in your Escrow.com account and pay Escrow.com. The transfer starts only after Escrow.com confirms the funds are secured.

      03

      Receive the domain

      We arrange the transfer through the registrar. You check that the domain is in your account during the agreed inspection period.

      04

      Complete the purchase

      Escrow.com releases the payment after your acceptance, or according to the agreed inspection and completion terms.

      Escrow.com is an independent service. Its account, verification and transaction requirements apply.
